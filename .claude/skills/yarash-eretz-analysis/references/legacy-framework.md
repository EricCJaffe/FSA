## Tax-Advantaged Inheritance (Stepped-Up Basis)

When heirs inherit appreciated assets, they receive them at current fair market value as the new cost basis. This wipes out accumulated capital gains tax liability at death.

Applies to:
- Real estate held until death
- Public securities (stocks, ETFs) held until death
- Most other appreciated investment assets

**Implication:** Scenic held until death = heirs receive it at whatever future value with zero capital gains tax on the appreciation. Same benefit applies to S&P holdings.

This argues *against* selling appreciated assets late in life if the holder is simply optimizing inheritance.

## Property vs. Stock as Legacy Assets

Family offices often mix both because they serve different purposes:

### Real Estate as Teaching Tool
- Tangible — heirs see, touch, understand
- Teaches responsibility (tenant management, maintenance, stewardship)
- Cash flow creates predictable dividend-like income for heirs
- Forces some active engagement with the asset

### Public Securities as Legacy Asset
- Easier to divide among multiple heirs (just split the account)
- Easier for heirs who don't want property management
- Simpler liquidity for estate settlement
- No property-specific risks (hurricane, bad tenant, etc.)

### The Active Management Question

Who manages the portfolio after Eric and Mary Jo?
- If biological heirs are interested in real estate: properties are powerful teaching tools
- If heirs prefer passive assets: liquid securities are easier to pass
- If no heirs are involved in family real estate: consider transitioning toward public markets over time

## Kingdom-First Reframe

The Jaffe priority matrix places Impact fourth — not last, but after God, Health, and Family. "Next generation" for a Christ-centered family may include:
- Biological descendants (children, grandchildren)
- Ministries funded or supported
- Churches planted or seeded
- Disciples made
- Kingdom infrastructure that outlasts any one family line

Don't over-optimize for biological descendants if God's plan is broader. A $30-50K/yr giving capacity compounded over 20 years can seed many more Kingdom assets than a marginally larger inheritance.

**Scripture reference for context (not as proof-text):** "Do not store up for yourselves treasures on earth... but store up for yourselves treasures in heaven." (Matt 6:19-20). The question isn't whether to pass more or less to children — it's whether the portfolio serves multiplication of Kingdom impact during this season and future seasons.
