### Why Each Expense Changes

- **Cleaning eliminated** — no turnovers between guests
- **Housewares eliminated** — tenant brings own
- **Insurance reduced ~30%** — LTR rider has lower liability/content exposure than STR hosting strangers
- **Interest eliminated** — mortgage is paid off (was historical only)
- **Lawncare eliminated** — tenant responsibility in standard SFH lease
- **Management increases to 10% of rent** — but absolute $ stays modest; STR management was underbilled in prior P&L
- **Repairs reduced 40%** — far less turnover damage, though major fixes possible
- **Taxes reduced ~15%** — no more bed/transient rental tax
- **Utilities eliminated** — tenant pays all utilities

## Practical Concerns for LTR Conversion

1. **Finding a qualified tenant** — Waterfront properties attract a different tenant pool than typical SFH. Screen for: stable employment, strong credit, references who actually answer the phone, willingness to deposit 2 months' rent. Shoreward has experience here.

2. **Insurance rework** — Need to re-quote as LTR (not STR). Expect 25-35% premium drop. Shop 2-3 carriers (Citizens is the FL fallback).

3. **Lease structure** — Start with 12-month lease. Option to renew based on tenant performance. Don't lock into 24+ months without proven track record.

4. **Boat lift / waterfront considerations** — Lease addendum specifying tenant's rights/responsibilities re: dock, boat lift, waterfront maintenance. Worth having an attorney review.

5. **Final vacation use** — Schedule any last family vacations at Scenic BEFORE tenant move-in. Emotional closure matters for Mary Jo.

## The Honest Recommendation

**Convert to LTR (Scenario 2 or 6).** Here's why:

- It's reversible — can revert to STR or sell later
- Stops the bleeding immediately ($18,810/yr improvement)
- Frees operational time (no more STR management)
- Preserves the asset and its waterfront optionality
- No tax event
- Tests the thesis with real-world data before making irreversible decisions

At the 24-month checkpoint, reconsider: if LTR is working and you want pure diversification, you can sell then from a position of strength. If LTR is working and you want to keep the asset, stay the course.
